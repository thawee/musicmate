# MusicMate 3.32.0

**Better-sounding volume leveling.** Leveling now changes the audio inside MusicMate, before headphone or car tuning, instead of turning down Android's volume. The new gain starts exactly where the next song starts in gapless playback, and 16-bit songs are sent as 24-bit so leveling adds no rounding noise. The default target is now -14 LUFS, like streaming services. Quick tuning on the playback screen picks Off, Track or Album and a pre-amp.

**Measure loudness.** Select tracks and choose Measure loudness to write ReplayGain tags into the files, with album gain when you select a whole album. An option in Settings tags untagged songs while the phone charges.

**Headphone crossfeed and a limiter light.** A Crossfeed switch blends a little of each side into the other, like listening to speakers in a room, so hard-panned recordings are less tiring. The tuning chip shows Limiting when MusicMate turns a peak down by more than 1 dB, and Clipping when a peak went past full scale.

**Mood queues.** A Mood chip in the queue keeps adding Drive, Focus, Wind down or Sleep tracks. Picking a source or mood now replaces what's next, with Undo.
