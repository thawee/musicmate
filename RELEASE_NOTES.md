# MusicMate 3.37.1

**MateTune visible on narrow phones.** On phones where the Output and MateTune tiles stack, MateTune was pushed below the playback controls, especially with a larger font, so only its title showed. Each stacked tile now shows its title and one line, and both stay on screen while the artwork above gives way and scrolls. With very large text (130% and up) the tiles still scroll with the artwork.

Also includes everything in 3.37.0: per-side speaker tuning, a Car, Room or Flat tuning target, measurement mic calibration files, and the restored car and headset play controls.

**Validation.** 497 app unit tests passed, and the playback screen was checked on an emulator at 1080x2340 and 360dp wide with font sizes 1.0, 1.15 and 1.3.

Version code: 167. See [CHANGELOG.md](https://github.com/thawee/musicmate/blob/v3.37.1/CHANGELOG.md) for details.
