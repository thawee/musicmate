# UX/UI review, 2026-10-02

Reviewed on a Galaxy S25 (SM-S931B, 1080x2340, 480 dpi, 100% text) running the installed
3.20.2-261002 build, with Poweramp playing. Screens walked: library list, navigation drawer,
Music Center (Playback, Queue, Server), Audio Output Picker, track menu, song detail, search.
Not covered: Artists/Genres/Playlists views, tag editor form, multi-select, Settings, full
screen console, 200% text, landscape.

The visual system is consistent and looks good (dark obsidian surfaces, gold accent, quality
badges, clear 48dp targets, good content descriptions). Most issues are about missing everyday
actions and technical noise that a listener does not need.

## Findings

| # | Pri | Screen | Issue | Evidence | Proposed fix |
|---|-----|--------|-------|----------|--------------|
| 1 | P1 | Drawer | Version is hardcoded and wrong: shows "v3.20.1" on 3.20.2 (3.21.0 now). | `MainScaffold.kt:220` | Use `BuildConfig.VERSION_NAME` (drop the date suffix). |
| 2 | P1 | Mini player, Now Playing, picker | Player app build number shown to users: "Poweramp • v1031". Reads like a bug and crowds the artist. | `PlayerNameUtils.java:167-219` | Show only "Poweramp". Keep the version in Diagnostics only. |
| 3 | P1 | Song detail | No way to play the song from its own page. Dock has Delete, Organize, More, Edit, Save; More has no Play either. | `08-tags`, `tag_more_actions_menu.xml` | Add Play and Play Next (icon buttons next to the title or quality row). |
| 4 | P1 | Library, search | No "Play all" or "Shuffle" for the current list or search results ("queen" gives 35 tracks; you can only play one at a time). | `09-search`; Play All exists only in RelatedTracksSheet and playlists | Add Play / Shuffle pills on the stats line ("35 Tracks • 1.51 GB"). |
| 5 | P1 | Track menu | Missing everyday actions (Add to Playlist, Go to Artist, Go to Album); "Open in External App" has no icon so it is misaligned. | `07-overflow`, `TrackListItem.kt:~312` | Play Now, Play Next, Add to Queue, Add to Playlist / Go to Artist, Go to Album / Convert Format, Open in External App, all with icons. |
| 6 | P2 | Library top bar | The Music Center button uses a bar chart icon, which reads as statistics. | `01-main`, content-desc "Open Music Center" | Use a now-playing / speaker-group icon. |
| 7 | P2 | Queue | Source chips (Manual, New, Downloads, Unplayed, Rediscover) overflow; the last two are off screen with no hint, and Replenish sits inside the scrolling row. "Downloads · 20/20" is unexplained. "256" appears twice (tab badge and header). | `04-queue` | Move Replenish to the header icons; add a fading edge to the chip row; label the counter ("20 of 20 added") or drop it; remove the duplicate count. |
| 8 | P2 | Server tab | Status and clients are one monospace paragraph; the IP wraps mid-address ("192.168.1" / ".58") and a browser shows as "Mozilla". | `05-server` | Status line on its own; clients as rows ("Web browser • 192.168.1.58", TV names as today), no mid-token wraps. |
| 9 | P2 | Output picker | The phone shows as "SM-S931B • Direct Hardware Output": model code and jargon, and the current route (speaker, Bluetooth, USB DAC) is not shown. | `06-picker` | "This phone" with the live route as subtitle ("Bluetooth • WH-1000XM5", "USB DAC", "Speaker"). |
| 10 | P2 | Now Playing | Artist line is low contrast over bright album art; album name is not shown at all. | `03-nowplaying` | Stronger bottom scrim under the text; add "Artist • Album". |
| 11 | P3 | Now Playing | Seek bar (thin, blue) and volume bar (thick, teal) sit together with different styles, so they are easy to confuse. | `03-nowplaying` | Give them one style family with clearly different weights, or label the volume row with its percent. |
| 12 | P3 | Song detail | Delete sits in the primary row next to Organize, one tap from a common action. | `08-tags` | Move Delete into More (it already confirms, so this is about mis-taps, not safety). Needs your call: ADR-013 placed it there. |
