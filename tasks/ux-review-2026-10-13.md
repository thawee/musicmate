# UX/UI review, 2026-10-13

Scored review (design-review rubric, Nielsen heuristics, WCAG 2.2) of 3.27.0-261013, walked
on a Galaxy S25 (1080x2340, 480 dpi, 100% text) with the real 8,117-track library and nothing
playing. Screens: library, Music Center (Playback, Queue, Server), output picker, studio
console, song page, drawer, Audio Quality. Song-info editor, Tech Info and Settings come from
the 2026-10-04 captures in `docs/screenshots/`; their cited strings were re-checked in source.
Code checks against HEAD `b7160bc3`.
Not covered: 200% font, TalkBack walk, Artists/Genres/Playlists, multi-select, Diagnostics,
and the queue while something is playing (#7 and #9 are from the Oct 4 captures).

Fixed since 2026-10-02: drawer version reads `PackageInfo.versionName`, the song page has a Play button,
the library has a Play pill, the Music Center icon is a waveform, and the picker says
"Phone Speaker". Remove Art and Clear Queue now ask for confirmation.

## Findings

| # | Severity | Area | Finding | Recommendation | Heuristic |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Critical | A11y | The DR13+ label in every library row uses `dr_high` #2E7D32 at 3.65:1 on #121212, which fails AA. The best-mastered tracks get the least readable rating. `TrackListItem.kt:226,230` calls `DynamicRangeMeter` without `highContrast`; only the song page (`AudioBadges.kt:240`) lightens it. | Lighten `dr_high` (for example #4CAF50 or brighter, then recheck), or default `highContrast` to true. | - |
| 2 | Major | A11y | The muted grays fail AA as text: #888888 on #2C2C2C 3.94:1 (13 uses, including `TrackListItem`, `FolderListItem`, `QueuePage`), #757575 3.62 to 4.07:1 (6 uses), #666666 2.9 to 3.3:1 (`NowPlayingPage`, `MediaServerPage`). | Use one muted token of at least 4.5:1 on #2C2C2C (#9E9E9E passes at 5.21) for all of them. | - |
| 3 | Minor | A11y | Transport icons are drawn at 36 to 40dp (`FullscreenStudioConsole.kt`), and several IconButtons at 32dp (`PlaylistPickerDialog.kt:281-315`, `MusicListScreen.kt:176`, `FolderListItem.kt:298`). M3 `IconButton` extends the touch area to 48dp, but neighboring touch areas can overlap. | Check spacing on the device. Where buttons sit side by side, use 48dp IconButtons and shrink only the icon. | H4 |
| 4 | Major | A11y | 17 places use text at 8.5 to 9 sp (badges, VU labels, the console), below an 11sp floor. | Set a floor of 11sp for any text with meaning. Purely decorative scale marks can stay smaller, but should be hidden from TalkBack. | H8 |
| 5 | Major | Consistency | The design tokens exist but are not used, and they have drifted from what ships: the token object's DR palette (six tiers) differs from the three `dr_*` resources that are actually rendered. The active `dr_*` colors are at `colors.xml:554-557`, next to two commented-out copies. There are 875 hex literals across 36 files. `QueuePage`, `MainScaffold` and the console each have 50+. Two brand golds compete: the library uses #FFD700 and Settings uses #FFB300. | Pick one gold. Route all colors through `MusicMateDesignTokens`, wire them into `MusicMateTheme`'s colorScheme, and add a lint/grep check against new `Color(0x` in `ui/compose`. Migrate one file per change, starting with the largest. | H4 |
| 6 | Major | Consistency | Quality tier names contradict each other. On Audio Quality, the Hi-Res Lossless tile is labelled "24-BIT STUDIO", while "Studio Quality" (SQ) is a different tier (24-bit, 44.1-88.2 kHz) that the library calls "24-BIT". The library pill says "HI-RES 24/96", but the song page for the same track says "HI-RES LOSSLESS" plus a separate "24/96" pill. | Use one label per tier everywhere, generated by a single function that maps a tier to its label. Drop "STUDIO" from the Hi-Res tile. | H4, H2 |
| 7 | Major | Hierarchy | Queue rows repeat what the section headers already say: every Up Next row has a "PLAY NEXT" pill under "UP NEXT · MANUAL PRIORITY", and the now-playing row has a "NOW PLAYING" pill under the "NOW PLAYING" header. | Show the provenance pill only where it differs from its section (for example, SMART rows inside a mixed section). | H8 |
| 8 | Minor | Usability | The queue source chips still overflow: the fifth chip shows as a 29px sliver at the right edge, with no fade. The header icons now have content descriptions (Load Playlist, Jump to Now Playing, Clear Queue), but sighted users still see bare icons. The count "7988" appears in both the tab badge and the header. | Add a fading edge to the chip row, and drop the count from one of the two places. | H6 |
| 9 | Minor | Now Playing | The seek bar follows the album's ambient color (blue on this cover) while the volume bar is white and thicker, so the two still look like unrelated controls (open since 10-02 #11). | Use gold for seek, keep volume neutral, and show the volume percent. | H4 |
| 10 | Minor | Song page | Confirmed on the device: the yellow Play button covers the album's logo ("EXTREME SIX" on #REBEL). | Move it outside the art (next to the title) or shrink it and inset it in the corner. | H8 |
| 11 | Minor | Tag editor | "Format" in the dock is ambiguous next to the "Convert Format" action; it could mean the audio format. | Rename to "Clean Up Tags" or "Tidy Text". | H2 |
| 12 | Minor | Tech Info | Overclaiming labels: 1000x1000 art is called "Ultra HD Master", and "STUDIO MASTER COMPLETE" really means all 8 tags are present. | Use plain wording, e.g. "1000 x 1000 · High resolution" and "All 8 tags complete". | H2 |
| 13 | Minor | Copy | "1 Hrs of Music" (library, drawer) uses the wrong plural, and units are mixed ("Hrs", "Min"). | Pluralize correctly: "1 hr", "16 min". | H2 |
| 14 | Minor | Picker | Confirmed on the device: the "Installed music apps" list cuts Poweramp off mid-row, with no scroll hint. | Size the list so it ends on a whole row, or add a fade. | H6 |
| 15 | Major | Console / queue | With shuffle on, the console says "UP NEXT: Tragedy" while the Queue tab lists #REBEL as track 1. The Queue tab shows list order, not play order, so it does not show what plays next. When idle, the console also shows a "CD QUALITY" badge and an empty pill with no track loaded, and the Music Center says "Select a song" with 7,988 tracks queued. | When shuffle is on, show the play order in the Queue tab, or mark it "Shuffled". Hide quality badges when no track is loaded. Make the idle line say "7,988 tracks queued, press Play". | H1 |
| 16 | Enhancement | Drawer | "Hi-Res Edition" next to the version is marketing text that carries no information. | Drop it, or replace it with the build date. | H8 |
| 17 | Minor | A11y | The studio console's Shuffle and Repeat buttons announce only "Shuffle" and "Repeat". The Music Center's buttons announce their state ("Shuffle, on", "Repeat, off"). | Use the same state descriptions in the console. | H1 |

## Suggested order
1. Quick wins: findings #1 and #2 (color value changes), #13 (copy, `StringUtils.java:532`).
2. Labels: #6 (one tier-label function), then #11 and #12.
3. Token migration (#5): one file per change, with a lint check against new literals.
4. Queue clean-up: #7 and #8.
